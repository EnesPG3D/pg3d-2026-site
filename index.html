About Pixel Gun 3D

Pixel Gun 3D (PG3D) is a multiplayer first-person shooter game with pixel-style graphics. Players can fight in online battles, complete campaigns, join clans, and unlock powerful weapons.

The game combines Minecraft-style visuals with intense FPS combat.

Main Features

  • Online Multiplayer Battles
  • Over 1000+ Weapons
  • Battle Royale Mode
  • Clan System
  • Custom Skins & Characters
  • Campaign Mode
  • Events & Tournaments

Weapon Categories

  • Primary Weapons (Rifles, Shotguns)
  • Backup Weapons (Pistols)
  • Melee Weapons (Swords, Knives)
  • Special Weapons (Flamethrowers)
  • Sniper Weapons
  • Heavy Weapons (Rocket Launchers)
